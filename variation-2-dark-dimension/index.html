About Us

A Family Business Built on Integrity & Pride.

ZED-AIR HEATING & AIR CONDITIONING INC. is a family owned and operated HVAC company proudly serving London and area for over 33 years. Started by Eric and Cindy Barron in 1993, we've grown into a multi-generation company dedicated to providing heating, air conditioning, fireplaces, ventilation systems, duct, and duct design services.

As Factory Authorized Carrier Dealers, our team delivers superior craftsmanship and exceptional customer service — ensuring optimal indoor comfort and air quality for every client.

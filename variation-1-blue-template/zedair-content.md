Mark's sister Dawn-Marie has worked alongside her parents for over 20 years providing accounting and administrative services while helping provide great customer service.

Kristen their youngest daughter has joined the team to help make sure the service department is the best in the area.

An organization's success is dependent on all team members. At the heart of our success is the dedication, support and hard work of all of our employees.

## FAQ QUESTIONS & ANSWERS
Q: What should you check before calling for service on your furnace?
A: Always check your filter — even if it feels like you just replaced it, check it again! Make sure furnace vent terminations are not blocked or plugged.

Q: What should you check before calling for service on your Air Conditioner?
A: Always check your filter! (80% of our calls are dirty filters!) Turn off breakers for A/C and furnace.

Q: How often should I change my filter?
A: One of the easiest preventative maintenance tasks you can perform yourself is to clean or replace your system's air filter. The air filter for your system will be located in or near your indoor unit. Whether it is a basic 1-inch filter, a larger 4-inch media filter — regular replacement is key to system efficiency.
